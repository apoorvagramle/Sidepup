Sidepup settings

A tiny golden retriever that sits wherever you park it. Your tasks, timer and settings follow you to any Chrome you are signed into.

Just updated? Reload any tabs you already had open. Sidepup only appears on pages loaded since.

You and the shelf

Where it sits

A task with a time reminds you at that time. A task without one is yours to tick off. Either way the real deadline is the daily reset — anything still open then carries to the next day, marked “yesterday”, and Sidepup mentions it when you start.

Drag Sidepup by the dog or by the bar to park it anywhere on screen. Bring it up to an edge and it rests there. Keep pushing past the edge and it tucks away and peeps out — click it to bring it back. Wherever you leave it, it stays there on every page, and on your other computers once Chrome syncs.

How alive it is

Need it gone right now? Press Alt+Shift+H to hide Sidepup completely, and again to bring it back. You can change the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts. The task list is only ever on screen while the panel is open, and the panel closes by itself when you click away, switch window, or leave it for 20 seconds.

Reminders and focus

These count unbroken work, not clock time. The count only runs while you are at the keyboard, and 20 minutes away resets it — so three hours straight earns a nudge, while an hour followed by a proper break starts you from zero. Nothing interrupts a running focus block; anything due waits for the break.

“Try it” fires that reminder right now, so you can check it works without waiting. It appears on your open web pages, not on this settings tab.
